# 评论分析结果

One reviewer reported that the Moto G7's IMEI came back as incompatible on every carrier they tried, including Cricket Wireless, Mint Mobile, and T-Mobile directly, and that customer service reps confirmed the device was not compatible with their networks. [d14d79450f3f_1]
Another reviewer stated that the unlocked Moto G7 works on all four major US carriers (AT&T, Verizon, Sprint, and T-Mobile) and their MVNO networks, and that they were using it on Google Fi with automatic switching between Sprint and T-Mobile. [14bf44a88dca_1]
A reviewer of the international edition described poor cell service connectivity on AT&T and T-Mobile compared with the domestic version, with no Wi-Fi calling available as a backup. [7cc9d91d904a_0]
One reviewer said they could not get a signal at home unless they went outside and could not get Wi-Fi Calling to work, then bought the US-warranty Moto G7, which worked perfectly with their Mint Mobile SIM. [9e96ef2d1bca_1]
A reviewer of the Moto G7 Plus international version reported it was not fully compatible with US carriers, particularly T-Mobile, with some calls going straight to voicemail and network type dropping from LTE to 3G or 2G during calls. [5d7f8b77d7f4_0]
Another reviewer warned that international models are missing some 4G bands for the five US networks (AT&T, Sprint, T-Mobile, US Cellular, and Verizon). [51b846eef7c6_0]
