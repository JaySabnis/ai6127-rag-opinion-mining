# 评论分析结果

Another reviewer reports that IMEI checks came back as incompatible on every carrier they tried, including Cricket Wireless, Mint Mobile, and T-Mobile directly. [d14d79450f3f_1]

One reviewer using a foreign SIM on the T-Mobile network reports the phone shows only 3G rather than 4G. [ed52e16a2a48_0]

A reviewer reports the phone worked on a T-Mobile MVNO but would not make or receive voice calls on an AT&T MVNO until carrier-side settings were changed to enable VoLTE. [246ab28b4231_0]
