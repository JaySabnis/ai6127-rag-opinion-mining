How to label

Read the whole chunk and pick one label for it. Press the number key (or click); the next chunk opens automatically. ← → move back and forth, so you can fix a label any time.

1 Positive

Only positive opinions (about the phone, its features, the seller/service or the carrier).
"Battery easily lasts two days. Love it."

2 Negative

Only negative opinions.
"Screen cracked after a week. Do not buy."

3 Mixed

Both a clear positive and a clear negative opinion.
"Small screen somewhat limiting but great for travel."

4 Neutral (no opinion)

No opinion at all: only facts, questions, wishes, or flat words like "moderate", "nothing special", "not an issue".
"Bought it for my mother last month."
"I would like at least a 4 hr battery life."

5 Unusable

Not English, gibberish, or not about a phone at all. Use rarely.

Rules

  • Facts + one opinion → the opinion wins. "Got it Monday. Works great." is Positive.
  • A clear complaint or praise counts even without opinion words: "Died after two months." is Negative.
  • A wish alone is not an opinion: "I wish it had more storage." is Neutral.
  • Comparisons: "Much better than my old LG" is Positive about this phone.
  • Sarcasm: label what the reviewer means. "Great, it died in 2 days." is Negative.
  • Judge the text only. The star rating is hidden on purpose.
  • Do not discuss labels with other annotators until everyone is done.
  • Unsure? Pick your best guess and move on (about 15–30 s per chunk).
